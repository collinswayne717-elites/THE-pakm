Who we are

A foundation for people and possibility.

The Palm Foundation Kenya is an emerging community-focused non-governmental initiative with an ambition to support people facing social and economic barriers. We aim to listen first, work alongside communities and build practical, locally led solutions.

  • Respect, dignity and inclusion for every person
  • Community-led and accountable action
  • Partnerships that make resources go further
  • Practical programmes focused on lasting progress

What we want to do

Our programme areas

These are proposed focus areas for the foundation. Activities will be confirmed and launched in consultation with communities and partners.

📚

Education & Opportunity

Encouraging access to learning, mentorship, life skills and opportunities for children and young people.

🌱

Youth Empowerment

Supporting young people with leadership development, skills-building and pathways to meaningful livelihoods.

🤝

Community Wellbeing

Working with local partners to identify needs and connect vulnerable households with appropriate support.

💼

Livelihoods & Skills

Promoting practical skills, entrepreneurship awareness and community-based economic resilience.

🛡️

Protection & Inclusion

Promoting dignity, safety, inclusion and awareness of support pathways for people at risk.

🌍

Community Development

Bringing residents, volunteers, institutions and partners together around locally identified priorities.
